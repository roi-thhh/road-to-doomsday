# Release Notes - v1.0.0 (Initial Release) ⚡

Welcome to the absolute first release of **Road to Doomsday**! This version establishes the foundation for tracking your Marvel Cinematic Universe rewatch journey leading up to *Avengers: Doomsday* in May 2026.

## 🚀 What's Included in v1.0.0

### Core Tracking Engine
- **Complete MCU Database:** Track your progress across both the core "Movie Road" and the multiverse/street-level "Series Road".
- **Real-Time Partner Sync:** Securely link your account with a partner using Supabase Realtime to track each other's progress live.
- **Dynamic Scopes:** Filter the massive timeline between **100% Completionist** (every piece of MCU media) or cut the fat and stick strictly to the **Doomsday Essentials**.
- **Flexible Sorting:** View the timeline in pure **Chronological Order** or exact theatrical **Release Order**. 
- *(Note: The app now defaults to Release Order & 100% Completionist upon first load).*

### User Experience & Interface
- **Neo-Brutalist Aesthetic:** A bold, unapologetic design system utilizing sharp borders, heavy shadows, and punchy `neo-yellow`, `neo-red`, and `neo-blue` accents.
- **Responsive Timelines:** Side-by-side timeline tracking on PC, gracefully degrading into an intuitive, side-by-side horizontal scrolling layout on mobile devices.
- **Doomsday Countdown:** A massive, live ticker anchoring the app, counting down exactly to the release of *Avengers: Doomsday*.
- **Interactive UI Nodes:** Dynamic movie and series nodes that visually update based on your (or your partner's) watch status.

### Technical Foundations
- Built on **Next.js App Router** for lightning-fast delivery.
- **Supabase Authentication & Postgres DB** for robust user tracking.
- Fluid micro-animations powered by **Framer Motion**.

## 🐛 Known Considerations
- To ensure partner progress sync works flawlessly, both users must be actively logged into their unique sync IDs.
- On mobile devices, the top control pane is intentionally formatted as a horizontally scrollable bar to maximize timeline viewing space.
